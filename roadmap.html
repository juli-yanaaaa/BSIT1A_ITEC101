04 / Plan your next steps

Our journey toward an IT career.

There is no single pathway to becoming an IT professional. The skills, training, and experience needed depend on your chosen specialization. Here is a general learning pathway to help you prepare.

01

Discover Your Interests

Explore IT fields such as software development, digital design, cybersecurity, data management, and networking.

02

Build Your IT Foundation

Learn computer fundamentals, operating systems, programming basics, and how information systems work.

03

Choose a Specialization

Identify the IT career that best matches your interests, strengths, and goals.

04

Develop Relevant Skills

Study the programming languages, technologies, and professional skills used in your chosen field.

05

Practice Through Projects

Create simple applications, websites, designs, or other projects to apply your knowledge.

06

Gain Experience

Participate in internships, school projects, workshops, or technology-related activities.

07

Prepare for Job Opportunities

Build a résumé and portfolio, practice interviews, and explore internships or entry-level positions.

08

Continue Professional Growth

Keep learning, improve your abilities, and explore new technologies and advanced career opportunities.

Suggested learning sequences

Career pathway examples.

These examples show how beginners might progress toward different IT professions. They are suggested learning sequences, not guaranteed job routes.

Software and Web Development

Learn programming basics → Study HTML, CSS, JavaScript, or another relevant language → Build projects → Apply for junior developer opportunities.

UI/UX and Digital Design

Learn design principles → Practice digital design tools → Create interface designs and prototypes → Build a portfolio → Explore junior design positions.

Data and Database

Learn data fundamentals → Practice spreadsheets and SQL → Develop data projects → Explore database or entry-level data positions.

Networking and Cloud

Study computer systems → Learn networking fundamentals → Practice configuring networks → Explore IT support or junior networking positions → Develop cloud skills.

Cybersecurity

Learn computer and network fundamentals → Study security concepts → Practice in authorized security labs → Explore entry-level security or related IT roles.

IT Support and Management

Learn hardware and software troubleshooting → Practice assisting users → Gain IT support experience → Develop leadership and project management skills.
