Understanding roles and responsibilities

An IT professional's role refers to their job position or main function within an organization. Their responsibilities are the activities and duties they are expected to perform. For example, a Web Developer's role is to develop websites, while their responsibilities may include writing code, testing web pages, and fixing errors.

Matching skills with IT careers

Software developers need programming and problem-solving abilities, while UI/UX designers benefit from creativity and an understanding of user needs. Cybersecurity professionals need security knowledge and analytical thinking, while IT support specialists rely on troubleshooting and communication skills.
