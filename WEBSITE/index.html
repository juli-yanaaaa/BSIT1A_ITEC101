01 / Get to know IT

What is Information Technology?

Information Technology (IT) focuses on using computers, software, networks, and other digital technologies to manage information and support the needs of organizations.

⌘

Technology that makes a difference

IT plays an important role in developing digital solutions, improving communication, and making everyday tasks easier and more efficient.

▣

Where IT professionals work

IT professionals work with software applications, databases, information systems, computer networks, and digital services.

Why it matters

IT is part of everyday life.

From online learning and digital payments to hospital records and online shopping, IT helps people and organizations perform tasks more conveniently.

🎓 EducationOnline learning platforms and student record systems.
✚ HealthcareHospital management systems and electronic patient records.
▤ BankingMobile banking applications and secure online transactions.
▦ BusinessSystems for managing operations, employees, and customers.
🏛 GovernmentOnline public services and digital government platforms.
🛍 RetailE-commerce websites, online orders, and sales management.

IT professionals develop systems, maintain technology, protect information, and solve technical problems. The industry offers different career paths for people with different talents and interests.
