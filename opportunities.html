05 / Start while you're a student

Opportunities for IT students.

Students can begin preparing for their careers even before graduation by building experience and exploring different learning opportunities.

✎

School and Personal Projects

Create applications, websites, designs, or data projects to apply what you have learned and build a portfolio.

⌁

Internships and Workshops

Join internships, technology workshops, and school activities to gain practical experience and learn from others.

◎

Online Courses

Continue learning through online courses and practice the skills used in your chosen IT specialization.

After gaining relevant skills, students may explore opportunities in companies, government institutions, educational organizations, technology service providers, or freelance work.

Your IT career begins with curiosity.

You do not need to know everything when you start. Explore different fields, discover what interests you, and continue improving your skills. Every project and learning experience can bring you closer to your career goals.
